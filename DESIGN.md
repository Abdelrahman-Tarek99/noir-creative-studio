# NOIR — Design Direction

## Positioning
Independent fictional studio: identity, editorial digital experiences, visual storytelling. Aim for restrained but unmistakable visual character. Portfolio-first, commercially relevant.

## Audience
Founders, boutique brands, digital agencies and marketing teams commissioning custom websites.

## Art direction
- Mood: editorial, minimal, confident, warm monochrome.
- Palette: paper `#F4F2ED`, ink `#171716`, fine line `#D3D0C9`, accent terracotta `#A65135`.
- Typography: large tightly spaced sans display with selective italic serif contrast. Replace system font with licensed/hostable fonts later without sacrificing performance.
- Grid: wide margins, disciplined asymmetry, thin separators, intentional whitespace.
- Imagery: original/licensed abstract sculpture, architecture, print design and studio still lifes. No generic AI-looking hero art.
- Motion: meaningful GSAP reveals and section pacing; subtle hover, restrained parallax; Lenis only when acceptable on actual devices.
- Accessibility: semantic headings, focus visibility, keyboard nav, WCAG AA contrast, reduced-motion alternatives.

## Pages
1. `/` — introduction, three selected projects, manifesto, services, CTA.
2. `/work/[slug]` — case study with role, challenge, solution, process, images, result (never fake metrics).
3. `/about` — positioning, approach, services.
4. `/contact` — validated working form or owner email; no fake submission success.

## Release quality bar
No layout overflow at 375/768/1024/1440px; accessible tap targets; stable images; performance measured on mobile; no broken links; real contact path; original/licensed assets; no false client claims.
